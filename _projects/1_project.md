Have you ever wondered how fish see in the deep-sea? As light attenuates through water, the available light spectrum rapidly diminshes and is eventually lost. 

Rockfish (<i>Sebastes</i>) is an adaptive radiation of marine fishes that inhabit a wide range of light environments (0-1500m).
